# Flight Day/Night Calculator – Privacy Policy

Flight Day/Night Calculator is designed with privacy in mind. The app does not require a user account and does not operate developer-hosted servers to collect flight data.

## On-Device Data

All flight calculations, airport searches, astronomical calculations and flight imports are processed entirely on your device.

Flight inputs, calculation history, draft inputs, settings and logbook notes may be stored locally on your device. This information is not automatically uploaded to the developer or external services.

Flight Import processes selected files locally. Exported results are shared only when you choose to use the iOS sharing features.

## Purchases and Subscriptions

Pro purchases, subscriptions and purchase restoration are managed through Apple's StoreKit 2 framework.

Apple processes purchase transactions and associated payment information in accordance with its privacy policies:

https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/data/en/app-store/

To determine Pro access, the app uses purchase and entitlement information provided by StoreKit, including transaction identifiers, subscription expiry dates, purchase verification status and original app version information.

Certain entitlement records are stored securely on your device using the iOS Keychain. These include entitlement status, lifetime purchase transaction information and cached subscription expiry information, allowing Pro access to be maintained where supported without an internet connection.

The developer does not receive your payment card details, Apple Account credentials or billing information through the app.

Purchases, restoration and entitlement verification may require communication with Apple's services. Core flight calculations remain fully functional offline.

## Support and External Services

If you voluntarily contact the developer by email or another support channel, any information you choose to provide may be received and used to respond to your enquiry.

Links to external services are opened only when selected by the user. Those services operate under their own privacy policies.

## Data Retention and Control

Saved flight history can be deleted within the app, and draft inputs can be reset using the app's controls.

Locally stored data is subject to standard iOS storage and removal behaviour. Keychain entitlement records may persist after the app is uninstalled.

Apple manages purchase and subscription records according to its own policies.

This policy applies to versions of the app distributed through TestFlight and the App Store.

## Disclaimer

This tool is intended for **personal logbook reference only**.

It must not be relied upon for operational flight planning, navigation, or safety-critical decisions.

The results depend entirely on the data you enter. Incorrect or incomplete inputs may produce incorrect results. Always cross-check outputs and ensure they comply with applicable regulations before logging flight time.

Estimated Uncertainty is a confidence metric for the modeled day/night split. It is not suitable for operational use.

Edge cases, particularly at high latitudes or near twilight boundaries, may produce results that differ from expectation. If something looks unusual, double-check your inputs.

Airport data is sourced from OurAirports and may contain inaccuracies or omissions.
